# Creator Preflight

Before public release:

[ ] What exactly is the product?
[ ] Who created it?
[ ] Is the source artifact identified?
[ ] Is it original or properly licensed?
[ ] Is any real person's likeness involved?
[ ] Is consent required?
[ ] Is family review required?
[ ] Is the intended audience clear?
[ ] Is the price explicit?
[ ] Is fulfillment defined?
[ ] Are gross revenue and profit kept separate?
[ ] Is there a correction / refund path?

FAIL CLOSED:

DRAFT -> HOLD
CONSENT_MISSING -> HOLD
SOURCE_UNBOUND -> HOLD
PUBLIC_RELEASE_NOT_APPROVED -> HOLD
