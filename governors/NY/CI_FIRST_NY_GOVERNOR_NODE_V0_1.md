## Gate 1 — Governing authority
New York Constitution Article VII §3 requires the governor to submit proposed appropriation/reappropriation bills to the Legislature. Legislative enactment, agency obligations and disbursements remain separate events.
Source: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/CNS/A7S3
