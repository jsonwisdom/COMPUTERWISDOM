## 1 — Minnesota / civil government

Minnesota is a constitutionally established state government inside the U.S. federal system. The official Minnesota Governor’s Office identifies Peggy Flanagan as Lieutenant Governor.

The office does not automatically absorb every gubernatorial power. Minnesota Constitution Article V, section 5 provides that the lieutenant governor becomes governor during a vacancy and that gubernatorial powers and duties devolve to the lieutenant governor when the governor is unable to discharge them. Minnesota Statutes § 4.04, subd. 2 separately permits the governor to delegate statutorily assigned powers, duties, responsibilities, and functions to the lieutenant governor through a written order filed with the secretary of state; constitutionally imposed gubernatorial functions cannot be delegated through that statute.

```text
OFFICE
→ CONSTITUTION / STATUTE
→ SUCCESSION, INCAPACITY, OR LAWFUL DELEGATION
→ SPECIFIC ACTION
→ PUBLIC RECEIPT
```

Reject:

```text
TITLE → ASSUMED POWER
```

## 3 — NATO / alliance

NATO currently has 32 sovereign member countries. NATO states that all Alliance decisions are made by consensus, and that a NATO decision expresses the collective will of the sovereign member states.

The 2022 Strategic Concept remains NATO’s current strategic framework and identifies three core tasks:

```text
DETERRENCE + DEFENCE
→ CRISIS PREVENTION + MANAGEMENT
→ COOPERATIVE SECURITY
```

Consensus does not mean that a NATO officer independently acquires the sovereign authority of every member state. Nor does a collective political decision mean every Ally executes an identical national act.

A more accurate implementation path is:

```text
TREATY / NATIONAL AUTHORITIES
→ CONSULTATION
→ CONSENSUS DECISION
→ NATO POLITICAL / MILITARY DIRECTION
→ DELEGATED NATO COMMAND ACTION AND/OR NATIONAL IMPLEMENTATION
→ OPERATIONAL EXECUTION
→ RECEIPTS
```

Article 5 makes the distinction especially visible: each Ally has an obligation to assist an attacked Ally, but each determines the action it deems necessary, in consultation and coordination with the others.

Reject:

```text
ALLIANCE CONSENSUS → IDENTICAL NATIONAL POWER
```
