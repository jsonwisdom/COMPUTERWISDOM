## Historical boundary

The jade-dice composition is cinematic, not an archaeological reconstruction of Sun Tzu's personal equipment.

Public archaeology supports multiple ancient Chinese dice traditions, including Warring States ivory multi-sided dice, Qin cubic wooden dice, Han ceramic/hexahedral dice, and later bone cubic dice. Jade is a well-attested elite material in Eastern Zhou / Han China, but this record does not establish celadon-jade six-sided dice with modern red/black pip conventions as standard Sun Tzu-era objects.

Sun Tzu is traditionally associated with the late Spring and Autumn period. Therefore use **late Spring-and-Autumn / Eastern Zhou-inspired military dress**, not a labeled Han-dynasty armor reconstruction.
