## Season 1

### S01E01 — SHOW ME THE EDGE
Air University / Maxwell education claim -> separate curriculum, doctrine, command, and execution.  
**Lesson:** `EDUCATION != EXECUTION`

### S01E02 — MAXWELL ATOMIC MAGNET
Atomic object: Agile Combat Employment (ACE). Replay the documented intermediate chain: LeMay doctrine development -> CSAF doctrine signature -> Headquarters Air Force operationalization -> unit implementation -> exercise / practice receipt.  
**Lesson:** an authority edge may exist, but it must be found rather than inferred.

### S01E03 — PROVOST ZERO
Treat the provost as the first academic-authority node, not a suspect. Trace policy / curriculum / assessment authority and stop before operational command.  
**Lesson:** `ACADEMIC AUTHORITY != COMBAT COMMAND`

### S01E04 — GEMINI SPLIT
CrissCross Air University and Auburn University academic leadership. Same title, same state, or partnership discussion does not merge governance.  
**Lesson:** `SAME TITLE != SAME AUTHORITY`; `PARTNERSHIP != COMMAND`

### S01E05 — DON’S ART OF WAR DICE
Take a public presidential strategy claim and separate book reference, rhetoric, policy, legal authority, implementation, and outcome. Do not infer policy causation from thematic similarity.  
**Lesson:** `STRATEGY RESEMBLANCE != STRATEGY PROVENANCE`

### S01E06 — PRESIDENTIAL SHOCK GLOVES
“Shock” one public presidential claim with Constitution / statute / appropriation / executive instrument / agency execution / judicial review / congressional or IG oversight. Shock the claim, never the person.  
**Lesson:** `RHETORIC != LAW`; `ORDER != AUTOMATIC LAWFUL EXECUTION`

### S01E07 — CRIMINAL JUSTICE INSTANT REPLAY
Replay a public criminal-justice claim from viral statement back through complaint / indictment / plea / verdict / sentence / appeal / pardon or other actual record.  
**Lesson:** `ARREST != GUILT`; `CHARGE != CONVICTION`; `ALLEGATION != FINDING`

### S01E08 — AMERICAN TIKTOKSHOCK FINALE
Reverse a viral clip: `CLIP -> FULL VIDEO -> TRANSCRIPT -> EVENT -> OFFICIAL RECORD -> AUTHORITY -> ORIGINAL SOURCE`. Add a DARVO-pattern sidecar only as a rhetoric classifier: `DENY / ATTACK / REVERSE`.  
**Lesson:** `DARVO_PATTERN != PROOF OF GUILT OR MOTIVE`
