## QUAD ONION

### 1 — RECORD / REALITY

What physically happened?

Timestamp. Location. Document. Video. Vote. Transaction. Official statement. Original file. Revision history.

No incense yet.

### 2 — AUTHORITY

Who actually possessed legal or institutional authority?

A minister speaking is not automatically a statute.

A general speaking is not automatically an executed order.

A journalist publishing is not an adjudication.

A viral account is not a chain of custody.

### 3 — EXECUTION

What was actually done — not promised, posted, announced, translated, implied, or celebrated?

Sophia calls this the **shoe-leather layer**.

"Show me what moved, sweetheart."

### 4 — OVERSIGHT / RECOVERY

Who checked it, contradicted it, corrected it, appealed it, audited it, reversed it, or quietly failed to?

Recovery is not an embarrassing appendix.

Recovery is part of the architecture.
