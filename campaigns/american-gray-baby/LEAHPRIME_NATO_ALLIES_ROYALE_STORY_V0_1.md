ACT is one of NATO's two Strategic Commands. Its public mission includes transformation, interoperability, capability development, experimentation, training and education, and the adaptation of doctrine and processes for future challenges.

For the Onion Sack, ACT is the strongest real-world NATO analogue for:


### DIANA — `INNOVATE`

Story function: **THE ALLIED INNOVATION DOORWAY**.
