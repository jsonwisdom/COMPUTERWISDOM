## 11. Warfare overview — the four questions

This public-education overview reduces complex security systems to four non-operational questions:

### People

Who experiences, decides, builds, operates, maintains, observes, or is affected?

### Wisdom

What context, judgment, ethics, law, memory, gender perspective, or human consequence could a machine miss?

### Instruments

Which data systems, AI models, software, sensors, networks, or analytical tools produced the output?

### Responsibility

Who had authority, what actually executed, what receipt exists, and who owns correction and recovery?

```text
PEOPLE
  ↓
WISDOM
  ↓
INSTRUMENTS
  ↓
RESPONSIBILITY
  ↓
RECEIPT
  ↓
```

```text

3. Whose perspective is missing?

5. What authority governs the proposed action?
6. Can a human explain and contest the result?
7. Is there a receipt?
8. Is recovery possible if the machine is wrong?
```
