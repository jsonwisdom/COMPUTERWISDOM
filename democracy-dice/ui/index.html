Democracy Dice

Demo mode. Every roll here is SYNTHETIC. STATUS=HOLD. AUTHORITY_CREATED=false. FINAL_AUTHORITY=JASON. No real civic, financial, or approval action happens.

Three six-sided dice pick one of 216 civic prompts. Each die uses rejection sampling: a random byte of 252 or more is thrown away and redrawn, so every face is equally likely.
