# Wisdom Index Blueprint v0.1

## Purpose

The Wisdom Index is the human review surface between verified information and consequential action.

It does not replace ALMS receipts, JOY meaning, or COMPUTERWISDOM publication. It coordinates them.

## Core Flow

```text
INPUT
→ OBSERVE
→ VERIFY
→ UNDERSTAND
→ CARE
→ DECIDE
→ ACT
→ LEARN
→ REPLAY
```

## System Questions

```text
ALMS           Can we prove it?
JOY            Why does it matter?
COMPUTERWISDOM How do we teach and share it?
WISDOM         Should we do it at all?
```

## Review Surface

Every proposed artifact should display:

- source and receipt status;
- known gaps and conflicts;
- affected people and privacy class;
- whether family review is required;
- AI contribution disclosure;
- named human reviewer;
- decision and rationale;
- correction and replay path.

## Five Public Questions

```text
TRUE?
USEFUL?
KIND?
REPLAYABLE?
DURABLE FOR 50 YEARS?
```

These questions support deliberation. They are not a mechanical morality score.

## Decision States

```text
DRAFT
NEEDS_EVIDENCE
NEEDS_CONSENT
NEEDS_REVISION
HOLD
ARCHIVE
AUTHORIZED_FOR_LIMITED_ACTION
AUTHORIZED_FOR_PUBLIC_DERIVATIVE
```

## Family-Sensitive Gate

A proposal enters family review when it affects:

```text
IDENTITY
MEMORY
DIGNITY
PRIVACY
CHILD SAFETY
COMMERCIALIZATION
LEGACY
```

Family review does not manufacture unanimous consent. Each affected person retains an independent perspective and may withhold permission for their own protected material.

## AI Boundary

AI may search, organize, compare, summarize, and draft. AI may not supply consent, determine family meaning, authorize execution, or make the final Wisdom decision.

## Portability

The index must remain representable in ordinary JSON and Markdown, readable without a proprietary service, exportable for offline recovery, and independently replayable from source records.
