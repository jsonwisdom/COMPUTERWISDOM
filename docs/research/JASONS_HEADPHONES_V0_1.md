## Current supply-chain finding

QCY H3S, UGREEN HiTune Max5c, and iKF King Pro were identified in the supplied China-first research as sharing or being associated with the JieLi JL7018-series ANC ecosystem. Important boundary:

`SAME CHIP / SAME PLATFORM ≠ SAME ACOUSTIC PERFORMANCE ≠ SAME PRODUCT`

Differences may remain in:
- microphone topology
- firmware
- acoustic chamber
- ear-pad geometry and seal
- driver tuning
- ANC calibration
- battery and power design
- quality control

## Purchase logic

Primary variable:
1. Real-world ANC effectiveness

Fit gate:
2. Ear-cavity size, seal, and long-wear comfort

Then:
3. acoustic quality
4. returnability / warranty
5. battery
6. maintainability
7. Bluetooth / codec extras

Marketing rule:

`CLAIMED -56 dB PEAK ≠ BOSE-LEVEL ANC`

What is needed for a stronger ANC comparison:
- frequency-dependent attenuation curve
- low-frequency rumble attenuation
- mid-frequency HVAC / road attenuation
- speech-band attenuation
- test methodology
- measured seal / fit behavior
