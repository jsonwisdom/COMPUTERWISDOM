1. Know the road signs

200 OK

The service delivered an HTTP response successfully. A public 200 does not prove payment occurred.

402

The requested resource requires payment. Check terms before authorizing anything.

HOLD

Stop when price, destination, network, authorization, verification or delivery is uncertain.

x402 v2 uses PAYMENT-REQUIRED, PAYMENT-SIGNATURE and PAYMENT-RESPONSE headers. This classroom only demonstrates their roles; it does not create signed payloads.

4. The family's safety rules

  1. Educational access stays free without wallet, money or blockchain identity.
  2. Children do not approve real financial transactions through games.
  3. Adults verify price, network, exact recipient, scope, refunds and consent before paying.
  4. Payment receipts and truth receipts are different evidence classes.
  5. Production x402 and wallet signing remain HOLD until independently verified.
