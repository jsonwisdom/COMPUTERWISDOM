How the mountains grow

Each learning receipt becomes a height-0 leaf. The new leaf repeatedly merges with the last active peak whenever both heights match. The remaining peaks are perfect trees with strictly decreasing heights from left to right. Eleven leaves produce 3 peaks: heights 3, 1, 0.

The bagged commitment hashes peaks from right to left. This classroom uses a declared, domain-separated SHA-256 recipe: H(0x00 || UTF8(exactLeafJSON)) for leaves, H(0x01 || leftDigest || rightDigest) for tree parents, and H(0x02 || leftPeakDigest || baggedRightDigest) for bagging. The digests are raw 32-byte values, not concatenated hex text. It is an educational protocol, not automatically interchangeable with other MMR formats.

CI-FIRST: Hash integrity proves exact bytes, not truth. Inclusion verifies against a separately trusted root. No wallet, login, signatures, subscriber verification, server, or public publication.
