## Source-bound timeline

- **1929 / 1930 / 1934:** the official RAF Mildenhall history says the British government purchased land in 1929, construction began in October 1930, the station opened October 16, 1934, and King George V officially dedicated it October 19, 1934.
- **1992:** the official 100th Air Refueling Wing history records activation at RAF Mildenhall on February 1, 1992 and host-wing responsibility.
- **2015:** DoD publicly announced a plan to divest RAF Mildenhall and relocate major operational units.
- **2020:** the later EUCOM posture announcement said roughly 2,500 Airmen at Mildenhall responsible for aerial refueling and special operations, previously scheduled to re-base to Germany, would remain in the United Kingdom.
- **2024:** an official USAF release recorded a UK Ministry of Defence purchase of adjacent land for base-defense/security space and explicitly said firm future-development plans awaited survey and assessment.
- **2026:** an official July 2026 USAF report records the Air Force Chief of Staff and Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force visiting RAF Mildenhall and highlighting the 100th ARW and 352nd SOW roles across European and neighboring theaters.
