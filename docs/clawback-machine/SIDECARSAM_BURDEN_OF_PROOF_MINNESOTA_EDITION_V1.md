## Burden of proof

Every proposition is split into testable atoms with source, clocks, authority, required receipt, observed receipts, contradictions, failed searches, unknowns, and disposition.

```text
RECEIPT_CLOSES_REQUIRED_EDGE -> PASS_OR_DELTA
RECEIPT_CONFLICT -> DELTA / CONFLICT
REQUIRED_RECEIPT_MISSING -> HOLD
SEARCH_MISS -> HOLD, NOT NEGATIVE_FACT
RHETORIC -> NOT_PROOF
SATIRE -> NOT_PROOF
```
