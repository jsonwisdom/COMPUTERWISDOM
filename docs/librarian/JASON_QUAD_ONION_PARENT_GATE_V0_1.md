## Parent mediation (human-approved gates)
1. **Prepare:** Librarian labels every scene and asset by theme, intensity, source, and intended age band; labels are advisory, not proof of suitability.
2. **Review:** Parent/guardian reviews exact media and chooses whether and how to share a child-safe adaptation. No inferred or blanket consent.
3. **Adapt:** Transform an original adult plot element into a separate, independently checked child-friendly lesson; redact disturbing details and personal data.
4. **Preview:** Adult and child experiences remain separately navigable; preview only shows explicitly approved child-safe assets.
5. **Revoke:** Guardian can stop sharing or revoke access; removal from live access cannot guarantee deletion of previously distributed public copies.
6. **Replay:** Keep a local/private minimal audit of approved version identifiers and a human review outcome; do not publish child identity, age, viewing behavior, consent records or family relationships.

No automated age inference, consent generation, parent-identity assertion, coercive injections, injections into people, or background surveillance.
