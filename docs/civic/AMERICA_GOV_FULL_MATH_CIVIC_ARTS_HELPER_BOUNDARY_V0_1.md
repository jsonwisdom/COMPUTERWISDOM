# AMERICA.GOV FULL-MATH CIVIC ARTS HELPER BOUNDARY V0.1

Status: DESIGN_PROPOSAL_ONLY
Authority: false

## Purpose

Define a public civic-helper pattern that uses official-source retrieval, receipts, temporal comparison, satire, parody, cards, movies, music, and other art without pretending that a citizen, bot, or artwork has diplomatic or governmental authority.

## America.gov Boundary

America.gov is treated as an official-government information gateway and source-finding surface.

`AMERICA_GOV = OFFICIAL_SOURCE_GATEWAY`
`AMERICA_GOV != AGENCY_OF_RECORD`
`AMERICA_GOV != UNIVERSAL_TRUTH_ORACLE`
`ANSWER != FINAL_AGENCY_DETERMINATION`

Full-math wrapper:

`QUESTION -> OFFICIAL_SOURCE -> DATE -> JURISDICTION -> CLAIM -> RECEIPT -> CONTRARY_SOURCE -> STATUS -> HUMAN_EXPLANATION`

## Intelligence Boundary

CIA and other intelligence-community public materials may be consulted as named public sources when relevant.

`CIA_SOURCE != SECRET_FILTER`
`CIA_ANALYSIS != JUDICIAL_FINDING`
`INTELLIGENCE_SOURCE != PUBLIC_POLICY_AUTHORITY`

Do not state that America.gov answers are secretly filtered through CIA unless a receipt establishes that architecture.

## Citizen Abroad

An American abroad may act as a helpful citizen, researcher, neighbor, artist, or informal civic explainer.

`AMERICAN_CITIZEN_ABROAD != U.S._DIPLOMAT`
`LAPEL_PIN != DIPLOMATIC_STATUS`
`HELPER != OFFICIAL_REPRESENTATIVE`

Formal diplomatic status or authority requires the relevant governmental role or appointment.

Safe public framing:

`CITIZEN_HELPER / CIVIC_AMBASSADOR_METAPHOR`

not:

`OFFICIAL_DIPLOMAT_BY_DEFAULT`

## ENS Before DNS

`HUMAN / ROLE / COUNTRY_CONTEXT -> SOURCE -> RECEIPT -> EXPLANATION -> PUBLICATION`

`ENS != DNS`
`DOMAIN != AUTHORITY`
`HELPER_ROLE != GOVERNMENT_ROLE`

## Civic Arts Layer

Allowed public teaching skins:
- satire
- parody
- Box D / Garbage Press-style original cards
- animation
- movies
- music
- comics
- games
- family-safe characters

Art may make a question memorable. It may not convert an allegation into a finding or a source into authority.

`ART != FINDING`
`SATIRE != OFFICIAL_POSITION`
`PARODY != DIPLOMATIC_COMMUNICATION`
`POPULARITY != TRUTH`

## Always-On Audit

The civic audit may monitor the project's own source receipts, date changes, broken citations, conflicting official pages, changed agency guidance, and publication state.

It must not become covert surveillance of citizens.

`ALWAYS_ON_AUDIT = SOURCE_AND_EVENT_CHANGE_DETECTION`
`ALWAYS_ON_AUDIT != PERSON_TRACKING`

Anomaly outputs route to human review and may be summarized on a schedule. They do not auto-accuse or auto-publish.
