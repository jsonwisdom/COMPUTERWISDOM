## Autopen authority sack

Public record presently supports this process map:

```text
PRESIDENTIAL DECISION
-> DECISION MEMO / ORAL DIRECTION
-> STAFF SECRETARY LOG
-> EXECUTIVE CLERK
-> PHYSICAL AUTOPEN OPERATOR
-> SIGNED OBJECT
-> IMPLEMENTING AGENCY
-> ARCHIVE / CUSTODY
-> OVERSIGHT
```

Known public-source anchors:
- DOJ OLC (2005): a President may direct a subordinate to affix the President's signature, including by autopen; physical signature operation is not the constitutional decision itself.
- House Oversight majority report (2025): alleges authorization/documentation gaps in the Biden White House and requests DOJ review.
- House Oversight Democratic staff memo (2025): disputes the majority conclusion and states witness testimony did not establish unauthorized presidential action.
- Majority report testimony summary: Neera Tanden oversaw autopen use for executive actions but did not physically operate the device; she described notifying Executive Clerk David Kalbaugh, whose staff would physically autopen documents. Her office tracked decision materials but not hand-signature vs autopen for each document.
- Majority report references draft 2021 autopen-use guidance associated with Staff Secretary Jess Hertz and deputy Michael Hochman.

Hard rule:

```text
WHO OPERATED THE MACHINE != WHO AUTHORIZED THE PRESIDENTIAL ACT
AUTOPEN != FRAUD
MISSING AUTHORIZATION RECEIPT != PROOF OF UNAUTHORIZED ACTION
```

## Delaware questions sack

Delaware is a jurisdiction/entity lane, not a wrongdoing label.

```text
ENTITY
-> FORMATION RECORD
-> REGISTERED AGENT
-> BENEFICIAL OWNER / CONTROL (WHERE LAWFULLY AVAILABLE)
-> CONTRACT / LOAN / TAX / CORPORATE SIGNATURE
-> BANK / COUNTERPARTY
-> TRANSACTION DATE / AMOUNT / PURPOSE
-> PUBLIC-OFFICE AUTHORITY EDGE
-> REGULATOR / PROSECUTOR / COURT / CONGRESS
-> FINAL FINDING
```

Questions:
- exact legal entity and jurisdiction?
- Delaware file number, formation date, registered agent, status?
- beneficial owner/control where authoritative records allow it?
- who signed the operative agreement?
- who sent/received the money, when, how much, and for what stated purpose?
- was the transaction during public office, between administrations, or outside office?
- what legal authority links a public official to it?
- what final finding exists?

```text
DELAWARE INCORPORATION != FRAUD
FAMILY RELATION != PUBLIC-OFFICE AUTHORITY
TRANSFER != BRIBE
SHELL COMPANY != ILLEGAL COMPANY
INVESTIGATION != FINDING
```

## Dual Onion Sacks — Gemini Forward + Reverse

Forward:

```text
PRIMARY OBJECT -> AUTHORIZATION -> EXECUTION -> MONEY -> OUTCOME -> OVERSIGHT
```

Reverse:

```text
LATER CLAIM -> EXACT WORDING -> DATE -> CLAIMED PRIOR ACTION -> PRIMARY RECEIPT -> AUTHORITY EDGE -> MONEY EDGE -> OUTCOME
```

`FORWARD_RESULT != REVERSE_RESULT`.

## Congressional inquiry packet

### Autopen
- Produce operative autopen policy/guidance for each challenged period.
- Produce decision memos, routing logs, staff-secretary records, executive-clerk logs, and signature-custody records for each challenged action.
- Identify the official who conveyed presidential authorization and the person/office that physically executed the signature.
- Identify whether approval was written, electronic, oral, or reconstructed later.
- Produce implementing-agency records showing reliance on the signed action.

### Delaware/entity
- Identify exact entities, formation records, beneficial ownership where legally available, bank counterparties, contracts, and transaction dates.
- Separate private-family activity from acts under federal authority.
- Identify any final DOJ, court, tax, ethics, or congressional finding; otherwise HOLD.

### Supply chain
- Produce solicitation, award, modification, statement of work, contracting officer, subcontractor disclosures, conflict certifications, payment records, and oversight findings.
- For AI, include model/version, contractual guardrails, authorized use cases, access controls, and audit logs where releasable.
