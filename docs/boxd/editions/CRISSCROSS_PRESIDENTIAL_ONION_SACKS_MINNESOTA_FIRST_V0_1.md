## Minnesota first — office / court boundary

Current official public sources identify Tim Walz as Governor and Peggy Flanagan as Lieutenant Governor.

Minnesota Constitution Article V makes the Governor commander-in-chief of Minnesota military and naval forces. The Lieutenant Governor assumes gubernatorial powers upon vacancy or inability of the Governor; war by itself does not automatically make the Lieutenant Governor governor or commander-in-chief.

The Minnesota Supreme Court is the state's court of last resort and exercises original/appellate jurisdiction conferred by the Minnesota Constitution. It is a judicial branch body, not Minnesota executive command authority.

## Quad Onion

1. **RECORD / REALITY** — what public record exists?
2. **AUTHORITY** — who had lawful power to do the named act?
3. **EXECUTION / MONEY / DATA** — what action, payment, contract, access, or implementation occurred?
4. **OVERSIGHT / RECOVERY** — what court, legislature, auditor, inspector, appeal, correction, or recovery path exists?
