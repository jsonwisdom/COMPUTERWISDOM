## Purpose

Test whether the documented overlap between Trump-family crypto financial interests and federal crypto policy can be connected by a reproducible policy-development or conflict-review record. Public belief, timing overlap, and financial benefit are not by themselves proof of causation, corruption, or quid pro quo.

## Receipt corrections

1. Reuters reports President Trump disclosed more than **$1.4B** in 2025 crypto income.
2. Nearly **$800M** of that disclosed income came from World Liberty Financial: more than **$520M** from token sales and more than **$250M** from sales of ownership interests.
3. The approximately **$616M** figure belongs to Reuters' estimate of Trump-family profit from the **$TRUMP meme-coin venture**, not World Liberty Financial.
4. Reuters separately calculated that World Liberty had funneled **more than $1.6B** to the Trump family as of April 2026. This is a different metric from Trump's personal annual-disclosure income and must not be merged with it.
