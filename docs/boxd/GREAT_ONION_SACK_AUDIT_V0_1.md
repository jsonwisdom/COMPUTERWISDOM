## Political Algorithm Transparency — FAFO

FAFO in BoxD means **Find Algorithmic Facts & Outputs**.

It does not mean retaliation or punishment.

For any political, civic, procurement, justice, media, benefits, risk-scoring, recommendation, ranking, moderation, or public-sector algorithmic claim, BoxD asks:

1. What system produced the output?
2. What version/model/rule set was running?
3. What inputs were used?
4. Which inputs are public, private, inferred, or unavailable?
5. What human authority accepted, rejected, or acted on the output?
6. What audit log exists?
7. What appeal/correction path exists?
8. Can the same inputs reproduce the same output?
9. Did a later model/version silently change the result?
10. Is a machine recommendation being mislabeled as legal or institutional authority?

```text
ALGORITHM_OUTPUT ≠ AUTHORITY
RANKING ≠ FACT
MODEL_CONFIDENCE ≠ BURDEN_OF_PROOF
AUTOMATION_GREEN ≠ CLAIM_TRUE
```

## Editions

### Minnesota Edition

Public-source roots include authenticated Minnesota law, public court records, public state/local records, audits, procurement records, and federal records when a federal nexus exists.

The Minnesota edition inherits the ALMS/ReceiptOS discipline but does not treat GitHub, hashes, AI, ENS, Base, or a mirror as state authority.

### European Edition

The European edition is a cross-jurisdiction public-source replay. Each country/alliance/institution keeps its own authority tree.

NATO material may enter only through NATO's public official-text/archive/public-disclosure surfaces or another independently public source. NATO ≠ national government; an Ally ≠ NATO; a national military action ≠ NATO action unless an official source binds that relationship.

### Mirror Rail

A BoxD mirror is a preservation object, not an authority source.

Minimum mirror receipt:

```text
source_url
source_domain
source_class
retrieved_at_utc
http_status
mime_type
raw_bytes_sha256
raw_bytes_sha3_256 (optional)
source_last_modified (if supplied)
source_etag (if supplied)
mirror_path
mirror_version
previous_mirror_hash
retrieval_method
robots_or_access_note
```

If raw bytes were not captured, the object is a **SOURCE POINTER**, not a mirror.
