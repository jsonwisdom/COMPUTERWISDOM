## Prime rule

> **DOMAIN TRUST = 0. RECEIPT CONFIDENCE IS EARNED EDGE BY EDGE.**

A federal domain establishes who published a record. It does not automatically establish that every proposition inside the record is true, complete, adjudicated, or independently reproducible.

```text
OFFICIAL_URL != UNDERLYING_FACT_PROVEN
PRESS_RELEASE != CASE_FILE
CASE_CLOSED != EVENT_NEVER_OCCURRED
CONGRESSIONAL_QUESTION != ADJUDICATED_FACT
NO_PUBLIC_DISPOSITION != THEFT
NO_PUBLIC_RESPONSE != CONCEALMENT
REDACTION != WRONGDOING
```

## Authority surfaces

```text
whitehouse.gov
= executive-branch statement / presidential policy surface
!= DOJ investigative record
!= court finding
!= cash-custody receipt

justice.gov
= DOJ publication / filing / release surface
!= automatic proof of every underlying factual proposition
!= judicial judgment unless the cited object is an actual court judgment/order

fbi.gov / FBI public statement
= Bureau publication surface
!= confidential-fund authorization receipt
!= evidence-custody receipt
!= proof of money disposition

congress.gov
= legislation / legislative history surface
!= executive execution
!= case-specific FBI fiscal authorization

Senate / House oversight records
= oversight questions, testimony, written responses, requests
!= criminal adjudication
!= automatic proof of the premise embedded in a question

OIG / GAO
= audit / oversight findings within stated scope
!= universal factual authority

COURT
= filing / order / judgment must be distinguished
ALLEGATION != FINDING
ORDER != FINAL_JUDGMENT unless final
```

## Cash Custody Pincer — reported Tom Homan $50,000 matter

### Publicly bounded propositions

```text
REPORTED_HANDOFF_OF_APPROX_50000
= BOUND_MULTISOURCE / CONGRESSIONAL_OVERSIGHT_REFERENCE

PUBLIC_STATEMENT_THAT_INVESTIGATION_WAS_CLOSED
= BOUND_TO_PUBLIC_STATEMENT

CRIMINAL_BRIBERY_FINDING
= NOT ESTABLISHED

PUBLIC_FINAL_CASH_DISPOSITION
= HOLD
```

The congressional record publicly asks what became of the reported $50,000, whether it was returned, whether it was tracked, and who made the close decision. A July 2026 written DOJ response declined to disclose non-public investigative and deliberative information and referred the cash-disposition question back to that refusal.

This means the public replay does **not** currently close the custody chain.

## White House membrane

A White House political or legal statement does not inherit DOJ evidentiary authority.

```text
WHITEHOUSE_STATEMENT
!= FBI_CASE_FILE
!= DOJ_DECLINATION_MEMO
!= CASH_DISPOSITION
!= COURT_FINDING
```

The July 2026 DOJ written response declined to disclose the occurrence or substance of confidential deliberations, including possible White House communications regarding the matter. Therefore:

```text
WHITE_HOUSE_INVOLVEMENT_IN_CLOSE_DECISION = HOLD
```

Do not convert a refusal to disclose into proof that the communication occurred, did not occur, or was improper.

## Congressional membrane

Congress appropriates broad FBI funding and conducts oversight. That does not mean Congress approved a specific undercover cash handoff.

```text
CONGRESSIONAL_APPROPRIATION
!= CASE_SPECIFIC_PAYMENT_AUTHORIZATION

CONGRESSIONAL_QUESTION
!= ANSWER

OVERSIGHT_REQUEST
!= RESPONSIVE_RECORD
```

## OpenAI processor membrane

AI tooling may:

- extract exact statements;
- normalize dates and actors;
- hash acquired bytes;
- compare versions;
- calculate custody gaps;
- distinguish transmitted / acknowledged / retrieved records;
- map authority and money edges;
- return `PROVEN | BOUND | HOLD | CONFLICT | REJECT`.

AI tooling may not:

- invent confidential-fund records;
- infer the contents of a sealed or non-public case file;
- treat an official domain as self-proving;
- turn a congressional question into a factual finding;
- turn case closure into factual erasure;
- turn missing public cash disposition into theft;
- create governmental authority.

## Standing order

> **Trust no office by logo. Trust no statement by repetition. Show the authority. Show the byte. Show the custody chain. Show the disposition.**

And for cash:

> **If the government says the case is closed, the accounting question remains simple: where did the government-controlled money end up?**
