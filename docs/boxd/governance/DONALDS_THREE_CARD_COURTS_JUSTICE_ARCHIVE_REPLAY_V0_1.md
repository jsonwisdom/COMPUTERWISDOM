## Three Card Courts visualization

The satire works only if the cards stay labeled correctly:

```text
CARD 1 — NEW YORK
SDNY criminal / business / Epstein-Maxwell records

CARD 2 — FLORIDA
SDFL Epstein legacy matters + separate Trump classified-documents case
NDFL = later Maxwell proffer venue

CARD 3 — WASHINGTON, D.C.
DOJ leadership / Special Counsel institutional layer + separate D.C. election case
```

The trick to audit is **not** 'which card is corrupt?'

The reproducible question is:

> **When a record, witness, or legal position moves between cards, who had custody, what authority applied, what changed, and what stayed the same?**
