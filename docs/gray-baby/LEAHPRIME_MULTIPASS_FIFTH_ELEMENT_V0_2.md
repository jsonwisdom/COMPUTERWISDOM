## Five-Element Replay

```text
O1 — RECORD / STRUCTURE
What record exists? What event is claimed?

O2 — AUTHORITY / COMMAND
Who had lawful authority? What authority edge is source-bound?

O3 — EXECUTION / MONEY / SYSTEM
What actually moved: data, money, hardware, orders, personnel, access?

O4 — OVERSIGHT / CORRECTION
Who can audit, reverse, correct, compensate, or stop the action?

O5 — LEELOO / HUMAN CONSEQUENCE
What happened to the human because the system acted, failed, delayed, or held?
```

The fifth element does not create a verdict. It prevents a technically complete system trace from silently erasing the person affected by it.

## Multi-PASS Mechanics

Every institutional claim may be replayed through five independent passes:

```text
PASS 1 — RECEIPT
Show the source and preserve provenance.

PASS 2 — AUTHORITY
Show who could lawfully act and under what authority.

PASS 3 — EXECUTION
Show what actually happened in the system or world.

PASS 4 — OVERSIGHT
Show review, correction, appeal, accountability, and remedy paths.

PASS 5 — HUMAN
Show the human consequence without converting it into a legal conclusion absent evidence.
```

A missing pass does not get silently filled.

```text
MISSING_PASS = HOLD
CONFLICTING_PASS = CONFLICT
UNSUPPORTED_PASS = REJECT
SOURCE_BOUND_PASS = PASS
```
