## Purpose and boundary

An independent speculative nautical-adventure universe branching from the 2025–2026 Adventures of Jason narrative: Jason builds a globally roaming, flamboyantly excessive mobile civilization that travels physically while a separately verifiable cryptographic constellation links its journeys, discoveries, media, research, and source records.

**This is NOT a modification to:** the already authored Adventures of Jason origins; JOY/AL/ReceiptOS Merkle protocols; existing signed receipts; historical roots; TriggerDeck canon; Supreme Seymour's frozen role or constitutional invariants; or private Drive documents.

**Two connected tracks:**
1. **Adventure franchise**: glorious exaggeration, oceanic satire, episodes, crew/quest progression, Garbage Press pirate-NASA newsroom.
2. **Marine systems feasibility**: naval-architect-reviewed candidate vessel, speed/range budgets, vessel safety, autonomous support systems, connectivity, research and living systems; unverified targets remain HOLD.

## ARGO ZERO: The floating civilization

Jason's improbable principal residence and mobile project headquarters.

Zones:
- **Bridge of Wisdom:** redundant navigational instruments, human command chain, AI decision support with manual override, secure global communications.
- **Merklarium:** private-by-default source library, version-controlled voyage log, scoped evidence capture and cryptographic verification workstation.
- **Garbage Press Newsroom:** satirical news studio with source-linking desk, broadcasting edit bays, floor-to-ceiling screens, and a ridiculous pirate-NASA set.
- **The Foundry:** robotics, repair bench, marine engineering workshop, spares and safe tool storage.
- **Ocean Laboratory:** instrument handling, sample storage, underwater ROV and diving support under qualified operating procedures.
- **Home Deck:** resilient long-duration living quarters, galley, health provisions, entertainment, indoor plant garden (water/lighting/humidity loads to be assessed).
- **Vehicle Bay:** tenders, robotic platforms, submersibles where engineered, launched with approved handling/stability systems.
- **Crew and Safety Core:** watertight subdivision, survival equipment, firefighting, electrical redundancy, drills, rescue and professional marine personnel.

**Narrative line:** "Nothing left ashore" describes the character's aspiration, not a promise that all property, archives, or physical vehicles can safely/legally fit aboard.

## Quest progression

- Level 0 — Recover the Argo. Enter an authentic source; preserve chronology.
- Level 1 — Recruit the Argonauts. Separate people, agents and fictional characters by role.
- Level 2 — Build the ship. Trade-off naval architecture against impossible feature demands.
- Level 3 — Captain's Audit. Reuse Seymour's four checks as an educational mechanism without transferring authority.
- Level 4 — Collect voyage leaves. Each voyage artifact gets consent, timestamp, source class and evidence status.
- Level 5 — Cross the ocean. Maintain human navigation authority and contingency plans.
- Level 6 — Assemble the constellation. Bridge verified provenance claims only, with incompatible tree profiles kept distinct.
- Level 7 — Challenge Poseidon. Press satire cannot replace engineering evidence or a financial receipt.

**Reward:** non-financial virtual learning badges. No on-chain trading, wallets for minors, private-source publication or automatically granted proof status.
