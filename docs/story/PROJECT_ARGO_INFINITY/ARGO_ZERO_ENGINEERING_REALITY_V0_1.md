## Owner vision

A genuinely liveaboard, transoceanic mobile home + research vessel + broadcast station + robotic workshop + archive + underwater exploration platform, with 50-knot sprint capability as a stretch target and dependable bluewater range as a separate fundamental requirement.

### Reality comparator

**Wally wallypower50X** official preliminary figures: LOA 15.4 m; optional maximum speed 54 kn, official cruising-speed range 280 nm on the 500R option, at the stated speed. Source: https://www.wally.com/en-us/Fleet/Model/p/20-336-676-PUB/n/Wally-wallypower50X

**Pelorus** is an example of a vastly larger global luxury craft: approximately 115 m, maximum 20 kn and published 6,000 nm range at 16 kn. Source: https://www.burgessyachts.com/en/buy-a-yacht/yachts-for-sale/pelorus-00000967

**Interpretation:** both high speed and large self-contained ocean endurance are individually demonstrated in different vessel classes; their combination in a very large floating home is *not established* by these examples. Size, hull form, payload and sea states change the problem.

### Mandatory gates before anyone can call it viable

1. **Design brief:** intended length, number of permanent occupants/crew, preferred operational waters, maximum cargo weight/volume, stability and handling mission, budget ceiling.
2. **Hull/propulsion:** naval-architect feasibility study of all-up displacement, sprint and transit operating envelopes; consider monohull, multihull, foil-assisted and tender-supported alternatives without pre-selecting a winner.
3. **Endurance:** numeric target for continuous passage at an *economical* speed, fuel and water capacity, power hotel load, food and spares, backup propulsion and port logistics.
4. **Life safety:** watertight integrity, damage stability, fire zones, survival equipment, emergency electrical power, medical response, weather operating envelope and crew requirements.
5. **Shipboard science:** ROV launch loads, moon pool if applicable, crane/hoist stability, laboratory safety, sample chain of custody and pollution prevention.
6. **AI / communications:** redundant independent navigation channels, satellite uplinks and offline mode; human crew owns steering and collision avoidance. No unsupervised safety-critical AI.
7. **Digital evidence:** offline-first evidence vault, signed provenance where appropriate, storage encryption, independent backups and bounded public releases.
8. **Privacy:** keep private family, asset, and wallet records off public GitHub and newsroom channels.
9. **Finance:** capital, maintenance, crew, flag state, insurance, marina, port charges, repairs and reserve budgets modeled separately from satirical story.
10. **Acceptance:** independent review, class/flag approvals as needed, yard plans, commissioning, stability trials and measured speed/range tests *before* any capability claim.

### Design alternatives (comparative options; not chosen)

- **Fast primary hull:** tests the 50-kn stretch but competes with heavy payload and endurance.
- **Long-range expedition mothership + fast 50-kn chase/tender:** separates bluewater accommodation from high-speed thrills; likely a more tractable trade-off, but feasibility remains unproven.
- **High-speed advanced multihull with distributed loads:** research route requiring serious engineering, comfort and logistics work.
