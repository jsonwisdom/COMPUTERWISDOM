# EPISODE 001 — THE BOAT THAT ATE THE BUDGET

**PROJECT ARGO ∞ / ARGO ZERO**  
**Genre:** Speculative ocean adventure / technology satire  
**Timeline:** fictionalized 2026; not a factual boat commission, purchase, sea trial, or government investment  
**Draft status:** HOLD / Jason final creative approval required

## Cold open

In 2026, Jason Wisdom reached a conclusion that humanity had somehow missed for ten thousand years.

**Living on land was a software limitation.**

Land had property taxes, neighbors, fences, meetings, zoning laws, and occasionally a person who wanted to know why the garage contained seventeen monitors, two robots, and a giant inflatable pirate.

Jason wanted a civilization with a rudder.

So he opened his notes and wrote:

**PROJECT ARGO ∞.**

Under vessel type he wrote, *ABSOLUTELY UNREASONABLE*.

Under mission he wrote, *THE ENTIRE PLANET*.

Under budget he wrote, *ASK POSEIDON*.

### The naval architect

"Captain, how big a ship?"

"Everything I own goes aboard."

"That's not a length. That's a psychological diagnosis."

"Put it under requirements."

"How fast?"

"Fifty knots."

"At what sea state? With how much fuel? At what displacement?"

"Yes."

The architect stared at Jason with the expression of a man who had just discovered that his invoice would have sequels.

"This isn't a yacht," he said.

"Correct. It's an update to the Argo."

### Launch day

ARGO ZERO emerged from its hangar like someone had told NASA to design a pirate ship, then given a movie studio veto power over the laws of nature.

The bridge glowed like a spacecraft. Beneath it sat a newsroom, an oceanographic lab, a robotic workshop, a library, a living garden, and enough server racks to make a data center seasick.

The onboard satellite array could see almost every horizon.

The Golden Fleece was nowhere in the treasure vault.

"Where's the gold?" asked the newest Argonaut.

Jason brought up a constellation of glowing dots. Each dot opened a source, a date, a claimed observation and a verification attempt. Some dots were green. Some were yellow. Several were red. The important thing was that none were lying about their status.

"The old Jason tried to bring the Fleece home," he answered.

"The new Jason is taking home everywhere."

### The press conference

Garbage Press interrupted the maiden voyage with a breaking-news banner:

**POSEIDON DEMANDS FINANCIAL DISCLOSURE.**

The God of the Sea accused ARGO ZERO of dangerous extravagance. His lawyers requested proof of every propulsion specification.

Seymour examined the submitted paperwork and concluded that the only engine currently verified was the one powering the coffee machine.

"Excellent," Jason said. "The coffee machine passes. The rest are HOLD until tested."

A wave hit the bow.

A screen flashed:

`SEA_STATE_UNKNOWN`

`CAPTAIN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`

`MERKLE_CONSTELLATION=DESIGN_ONLY`

"Good," said Jason. "A ship that's not afraid to tell me what it doesn't know."

### The oath of the new Argonauts

Every ocean crossing would leave a record.

Every discovery would carry its original source.

Every correction would become another chapter.

No treasure would be declared found just because someone painted the chest gold.

No AI would outrank a real captain.

And no sailor would ever have to say "trust me" when the evidence could be shown.

### The final scene

At the harbor entrance, a suspiciously large yacht owner radioed, "What the hell kind of boat is that?"

Jason smiled.

"Not a boat."

"Then what?"

"The next chapter."

ARGO ZERO turned toward the open sea.

From the newsroom, a final graphic appeared:

**THE ADVENTURES OF JASON: THE 2026 OCEAN ODYSSEY**

**NEXT EPISODE: THE INSURANCE COMPANY DISCOVERS MERKLE TREES.**
