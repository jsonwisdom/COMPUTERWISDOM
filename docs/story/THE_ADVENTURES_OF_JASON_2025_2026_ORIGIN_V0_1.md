## GAME MECHANICS — REPLAY, NOT PAY-TO-WIN

Each level starts with a **Jason Question** and generates a proposed **Quest Receipt** containing:

```text
quest_id
fiction_label / source_type
observed_at / event_at / received_at
source_repository / path / immutable_commit_url
claimed_scope / evidence_scope
replay_method / expected_result / observed_result
contradictions / missing_evidence
status: UNRUN | HOLD | CONFLICT | SCOPED_PASS
jason_release_decision: NOT_PROVIDED | APPROVED | REJECTED
```

**Points:** Award only virtual, nontransferable learning points for finding sources, documenting gaps, and reproducing verification. A correctly identified HOLD can complete an educational challenge; it cannot certify the underlying claim.

**Boss encounter:** The Fake Green Dragon claims, "It's on GitHub, so it must be true!" The player wins by distinguishing Git integrity, actual cryptographic verification, real-world truth, and human authority.

**Golden Fleece ending:** The strongest player is not the one who says PASS most often. It is the one who can show the receipts, recognize what the receipts do not prove, and let another player reproduce the route.
