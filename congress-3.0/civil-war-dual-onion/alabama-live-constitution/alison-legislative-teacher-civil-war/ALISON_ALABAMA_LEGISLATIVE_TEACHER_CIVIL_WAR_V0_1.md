## Lesson 01 — Alabama 1861

The Alabama Department of Archives and History preserves the 1861 Alabama Constitution, also described as the Secession Constitution. Its preamble records Alabama's claimed separation from the United States, and the document includes provisions on distribution of powers, the legislature, executive/militia, judiciary, education, banking, and slavery.

Teaching state:

ALABAMA_1861_CONSTITUTION = SOURCE_BOUND_HISTORICAL_OBJECT

Official historical source:
https://digital.archives.alabama.gov/digital/collection/constitutions/id/6/

## Lesson 03 — Present Alabama constitutional state

ALISON exposes the Constitution of Alabama 2022 as the current constitutional teaching surface.

Current rails include:
- Article III — distribution/separation of governmental powers.
- Section 42 — legislative, executive, and judicial branches are distinct; Alabama describes government as a government of laws, not individuals.
- Article XV — militia.
- Section 279 — legislators and executive/judicial officers swear to support both the U.S. Constitution and Alabama Constitution.
