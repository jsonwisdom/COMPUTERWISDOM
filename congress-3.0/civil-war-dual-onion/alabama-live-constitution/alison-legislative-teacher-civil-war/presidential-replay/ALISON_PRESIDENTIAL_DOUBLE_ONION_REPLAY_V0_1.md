## Onion A — Presidential Power

```text
PRESIDENT
→ EXACT CLAIM
→ EXACT ACTION
→ CLAIMED POWER
→ CONSTITUTIONAL TEXT
→ STATUTORY DELEGATION
→ AUTHORITY ON EVENT DATE
→ CONGRESSIONAL RESPONSE
→ JUDICIAL REVIEW
→ LEGAL AUTHORITY JAW
→ EXECUTION EVIDENCE JAW
→ LATER AMENDMENT / STATUTE / PRECEDENT
→ CURRENT STATE
→ BOXD
→ PASS | HOLD | CONFLICT | REJECT
```

The legal-authority jaw asks:

`ACTION → BASIS → AGENCY/EXECUTOR → DEADLINE/DURATION → FUNDING → LIMITS`

The execution-evidence jaw asks:

`GUIDANCE → CONTRACTS/ORDERS → DEPLOYMENT → TESTING/REVIEW → RESULTS → MISSING/CONFLICTING RECORDS`

Atomic question:

**What was legally directed, and what can be proven to have happened?**

## Onion B — People / Citizen Challenge Capacity

```text
RIGHT TO OBJECT
→ RIGHT TO VOTE
→ RIGHT TO PETITION
→ ACCESS TO COURT
→ ACCESS TO COUNSEL / PROFESSION
→ INSTITUTIONAL BARRIERS
→ RETALIATION / BURDEN
→ ACTUAL REMEDY
→ CHALLENGE EVIDENCE
→ BOXD
→ PASS | HOLD | CONFLICT | REJECT
```

This onion is date-bound. A right recognized today cannot be retrojected into an earlier challenge environment.

Historical exclusion is recorded as an access fact, not as proof that every excluded person was silent or powerless. Petition, publication, organizing, litigation, voting, office-holding, professional access, and actual remedy remain separate fields.

## Cross-Onion Receipt

```text
PRESIDENTIAL_AUTHORITY_STATUS
              ×
CITIZEN_CHALLENGE_CAPACITY_STATUS
              ↓
        CROSS-ONION RECEIPT
              ↓
     PASS | HOLD | CONFLICT | REJECT
```

The two onions do not collapse into each other.

A strong presidential authority chain does not prove citizens had meaningful access to challenge it.

A weak or exclusionary challenge environment does not by itself prove the presidential action unlawful.

## Source discipline

Preferred primary/official classes:

- Constitution text / Constitution Annotated for clause and doctrine navigation
- enacted statutes / Statutes at Large
- presidential proclamations, orders, messages, and archival records
- congressional acts, votes, funding, investigations, impeachment materials

Each source must carry a temporal role:

`PRE_EVENT_AUTHORITY | AT_EVENT | CONTEMPORARY_CHALLENGE | LATER_REVIEW | LATER_SUPERSESSION | CURRENT_STATE`

## Constitutional anchors for the schema

Article II creates the presidency and enumerates executive powers and duties; it does not make presidential identity a source of authority.

Article III case-or-controversy and standing doctrine constrain access to federal judicial review; nonreview therefore cannot be treated as automatic validation.

The Nineteenth Amendment changed the constitutional voting-rights environment by prohibiting denial or abridgment of voting rights on account of sex. Historical challenge-capacity receipts before that transition must not silently assume the later voting-rights state.
