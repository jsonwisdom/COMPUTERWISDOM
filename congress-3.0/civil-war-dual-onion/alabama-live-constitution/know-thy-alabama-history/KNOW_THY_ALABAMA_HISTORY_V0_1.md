## Rule

Know Alabama history by replaying claims through sources, dates, authority, consequences, and constitutional change.

```text
KNOW != MEMORIZE_A_NARRATIVE
KNOW = SOURCE + CONTEXT + AUTHORITY + CHANGE + RECEIPT
```

## Seven-question classroom kernel

```text
WHO said or enacted it?
WHAT exactly was claimed or done?
WHEN did it happen?
WHERE did it happen?
BY WHAT AUTHORITY?
WHAT CHANGED AFTERWARD?
WHAT RECEIPT lets another person replay it?
```

## Alabama History Dice

```text
SOURCE_DIE      = ARCHIVE | CONSTITUTION | STATUTE | COURT | SPEECH | NEWSPAPER | STORY
TIME_DIE        = BEFORE_WAR | 1861 | WAR | EMANCIPATION | RECONSTRUCTION | MODERN
AUTHORITY_DIE   = FEDERAL | STATE | LOCAL | MILITARY | COURT | PRIVATE
PEOPLE_DIE      = ENSLAVED_PERSON | FREEDPERSON | CIVILIAN | SOLDIER | OFFICIAL | COMMUNITY
QUESTION_DIE    = WHO | WHAT | WHEN | WHERE | WHY | HOW
REPLAY_DIE      = SOURCE_CHECK | AUTHORITY_CHECK | TIME_CHECK | CONSEQUENCE_CHECK | CONTRADICTION_CHECK
```

A roll selects a question. It never supplies the answer.

## Classroom replay

```text
ROLL
-> FORM QUESTION
-> FIND SOURCE
-> IDENTIFY DATE
-> IDENTIFY SPEAKER / ACTOR
-> IDENTIFY AUTHORITY
-> CHECK CONSEQUENCE
-> CHECK LATER CONSTITUTIONAL CHANGE
-> CROSS-COMPARE
-> PASS | HOLD | CONFLICT | REJECT
```
