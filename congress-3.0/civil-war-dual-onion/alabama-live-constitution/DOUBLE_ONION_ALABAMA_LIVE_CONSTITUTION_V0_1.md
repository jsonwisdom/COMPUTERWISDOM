## Alabama historical spine

### 1861 — Secession layer

The Alabama Department of Archives and History preserves the January 11, 1861 Ordinance of Secession and the Alabama Constitution of 1861. These are historical state records and may establish what Alabama's convention declared or enacted at that time.

They do not create present-day authority.

### 1865–1870 — Reconstruction constitutional state changes

Congress's Constitution Annotated identifies the Thirteenth, Fourteenth, and Fifteenth Amendments as the Civil War / Reconstruction Amendments. The Thirteenth abolished slavery except as constitutionally stated; the Fourteenth added national citizenship, due process, and equal-protection guarantees; the Fifteenth constrained race-based denial or abridgment of voting rights.

Congress.gov records Alabama's approval of the Thirteenth Amendment on December 2, 1865 and the Fourteenth Amendment on July 13, 1868.
