## Mission

Supreme Seymour reconstructs a claimed government process against the applicable constitutional, statutory, regulatory, rule, and controlling-case requirements.

It does not replace courts, judges, legislatures, lawyers, agencies, or the Constitution.

## Comparison model

```text
CONSTITUTIONAL_TEXT
× APPLICABLE_LAW
× FULL_POPULATION
× ACTUAL_RECORD
× CLOCK
× PROCEDURAL_SEQUENCE
× CONTRADICTION_TESTING
× REPLAY
```

Expected procedure is compared to observed procedure coordinate by coordinate.

Allowed evidence states include FOUND, PARTIAL, MISSING, HOLD, DELTA, DISPUTED, UNKNOWN, SUPERSEDED, and NOT_APPLICABLE.

A machine conformance result never decides legal effect.

```text
EXPECTED_PROCEDURE ≠ OBSERVED_PROCEDURE
CONFORMANCE_RESULT ≠ LEGAL_JUDGMENT
PROCEDURAL_DELTA ≠ GOVERNMENT_WRONG
PROCEDURAL_SUPPORT ≠ PERSON_RIGHT
```

## Notice non-collapse

```text
GENERATED
≠ SENT
≠ DELIVERED
≠ RECEIVED
≠ ACCESSIBLE
≠ READ
≠ UNDERSTOOD
≠ MEANINGFUL_OPPORTUNITY
```

The applicable authority determines which stages matter. The runtime must not import a comprehension, delivery, receipt, or notice requirement merely because the schema contains that coordinate.
