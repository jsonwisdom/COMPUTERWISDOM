His job is to take the thing that looks settled and ask whether the record actually replays:

- What happened?
- What was filed?
- What was ordered?
- What was only reported?

## Three-clock law

Every material court object should preserve at least:

- `EFFECTIVE_AT` — when the underlying procedural/legal state became effective;
- `OBSERVED_AT` — when the research process discovered it;
- `RECEIVED_AT` — when the replay system ingested it.

A lag between these clocks is itself auditable.

`EFFECTIVE_AT != OBSERVED_AT != RECEIVED_AT`

## Docket-state discipline

Court objects are replayed through declared lifecycle states rather than free text:

`UNKNOWN → FILED → SERVED → RESPONSE_PENDING → RESPONDED → ARGUMENT_SCHEDULED → ARGUED → SUBMITTED → ORDERED → DECIDED → REHEARING_PENDING → REHEARD → FINAL → CLOSED`

A replay may HOLD at any state when evidence is incomplete.
