# FILM I — THE LAKE NODE

**Time filter:** 1979–1981  
**Influence grammar:** industrial-space dread, cold analog machinery, bodily unease, bureaucratic silence.  
**Location:** fictional abandoned NATO-adjacent signals facility in Northern Europe.

A multinational verification team arrives to close **Lake Node**, a concrete communications site built around a lake whose maps never quite agree.

The decommission file contains one impossible sentence:

> `SUBJECT: GRAY BABY WISDOM — BIOLOGICAL ANALYTIC UNIT`

The sentence is **FILE CLAIM ONLY**. The film never establishes it as objective truth.

On the first desk inside the facility sits a hockey mask. Beside it lies a spotless machete tagged `INERT / RETAIN FOR INVENTORY`.

Nothing attacks the team. Instead, inventories change after being signed. Corridor numbers duplicate. Audio reels contain debriefs recorded before the team arrived. A cabin wall appears behind poured concrete as if an American summer camp has been physically archived inside European brutalism.

### Documentary artifacts

1. **Decommission Order 7-LN** — conflicting page counts.
2. **Desk Photograph 001** — mask + inert machete, timestamp disputed.
3. **Tape LN-A** — room tone followed by a child-sized respirator sound.
4. **Floor Plan Revision C** — includes a wooden mess hall absent from the building.
5. **Witness Sheet** — every signature valid; no witness remembers signing together.

### Ending

The team seals the site. The final shot remains inside after the door closes. A reel-to-reel machine starts by itself and plays the sound of a bus braking on gravel.

# FILM II — THE REFUSAL

**Time filter:** 1983–1985  
**Influence grammar:** administrative dystopia, empty-city austerity, dead technology, institutional absurdity.

A formal non-acknowledgment enters the story-world archive:

> `NO FACILITY MATCHING LAKE NODE EXISTS IN THE REFERENCED RECORD SERIES.`

This is a **fictional prop**, not a real NATO statement.

The denial should close the case. Instead, every office receiving the notice develops a different version of the Lake Node file. Some versions contain photographs of teenagers at a summer camp. Others contain transport manifests for buses that were never scheduled.

Then the buses arrive.

The teenagers know the names of cabins that do not exist in Europe. They complain about counselors nobody hired. They insist the lake has always been called Crystal Lake, while every institutional sign says something different.

Gray Baby is rarely seen directly. It appears in reflections, ID photos, photocopy artifacts, and one frame of degraded security footage. It never performs an attack.

**V-1 is active:** persistence through records, architecture, and repetition.

### Documentary artifacts

1. **NONACK-83** — denial receipt with contradictory routing stamps.
2. **Bus Manifest B-13** — passenger names duplicated from obsolete personnel files.
3. **Camp Orientation Cassette** — American voice, European electrical hum.
4. **Photocopied Mask Diagram** — labeled `INTERFACE / DO NOT WEAR`.
5. **Maintenance Ticket 44** — complaint: “lake returning through basement wall.”

### Ending

An administrator stamps the final refusal.

The stamp reads:

`CLOSED`.

On the reverse side, already printed before the stamp lands:

`REOPENED`.

# FILM III — THE REPLAY

**Time filter:** 1986–1989  
**Influence grammar:** decayed science fiction, circular institutions, absurdist survival, civilization running on broken forms.

Lake Node can no longer be cleanly separated into building, archive, memory, or story.

The facility is now the entity.

The entity is now the facility.

Every corridor returns to the lake.

Investigators stop asking, “What happened here?” and begin asking, “Which version can be replayed without inventing authority?”

Gray Baby finally appears in full view. It lifts the hockey mask and puts it on.

Not as disguise.

As **interface**.

The mask contains no magic and grants no power. It functions as the visual provenance tag connecting incompatible versions of the story.

The machete remains spotless, catalogued, and unused.

### Replay protocol

```text
1. Preserve original artifact.
2. Label source class.
3. Separate observation from interpretation.
4. Mark contradictions instead of resolving them by force.
5. Replay only what the record supports.
6. Never convert repetition into proof.
7. Never convert a file label into identity authority.
```

### Documentary artifacts

1. **Replay Deck R-1** — synchronized film, audio, floor plan, receipt sequence.
2. **Mask Inventory Card** — same serial number, three incompatible manufacture dates.
3. **Lake Photograph Set** — shoreline geometry changes, camera position does not.
4. **Clean Machete Receipt** — continuously inventoried; no use established.
5. **Final Operator Log** — contains only: `REPLAY COMPLETE / ORIGIN UNRESOLVED`.

### Ending

Gray Baby walks down the final corridor and reaches the original desk from Film I.

The mask is already there.

Gray Baby is still wearing it.

Cut to the lake.

A bus can be heard, but never seen.
