## ARCHIVE FINDING

Quantum teleportation does not teleport matter, people, messages, or usable information faster than light.

It transfers an unknown quantum state by consuming:

- one pre-shared entangled pair,
- a Bell-state measurement, and
- two classical bits that must reach the receiver normally.

The original state is destroyed. Bob cannot reconstruct it until Alice’s classical result arrives. The 1993 Bennett–Brassard–Crépeau–Jozsa–Peres–Wootters protocol remains the canonical foundation.

---

## QUANTUM STACK

**TELEPORTATION**  
Transfers a quantum state across pre-established entanglement.

**ENTANGLEMENT SWAPPING**  
Joins two shorter entangled links by measuring their intermediate qubits.

**QUANTUM REPEATER**  
Attempts to combine entanglement generation, memory, swapping, synchronization, and error management into a longer end-to-end quantum link.

**QKD**  
Generates shared key material while allowing channel disturbances to be detected under a defined protocol and security model.

---

## CRITICAL CORRECTION

QKD offers strong physics-based security proofs, but practical equipment is not automatically invulnerable. Sources, detectors, and implementation details remain attack surfaces.

Quantum repeaters remain developmental.
Continental-scale quantum internet is not operational.

DOE and NIST describe quantum repeaters as technology being developed to distribute entanglement over longer distances—not as a mature replacement for existing fiber, radio, or satellite networks.
