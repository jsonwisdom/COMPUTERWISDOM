## Purpose

Turn institutional replay into a television-style public-education segment that makes contradictions difficult to hand-wave away.

`Boomers` is a rhetorical audience label for the satire format. It does not authorize discrimination, humiliation, coercion, or physical treatment based on age.

## Format

```text
HOST READS CLAIM
↓
SOURCE CARD APPEARS
↓
FOUR ONIONS OPEN
↓
LEELOO MULTI PASS RUNS
↓
CONTRADICTION = SCREEN / AUDIO STINGER
↓
FAFO ATTACHMENT SHOWS CONSEQUENCE
↓
PUBLIC RECEIPT / QR / SOURCE ID
```

The word `shock` describes the editorial reveal of a contradiction, not a device applied to a person.

## On-screen Four Onion cards

### 🧅 Record

Display timestamps, source IDs, transcripts, docket entries, public GeoJSON or location records when lawfully public, hashes, version history, and contradictions.

### 🧅 Authority

Display office, jurisdiction, constitutional/statutory authority, delegation, limits, and review path.

### 🧅 Execution

Display appropriation, program, contract, invoice, personnel/access, travel, policy, implementation, and consequence when source-bound.

### 🧅 Oversight

Display OIG/IG, Congress/GAO, court orders, appeals, public-access status, corrections, and unresolved edges.

## LeeLoo Multi Pass display

```text
O1 RECORD      [PASS|HOLD|CONFLICT|REJECT]
O2 AUTHORITY   [PASS|HOLD|CONFLICT|REJECT]
O3 EXECUTION   [PASS|HOLD|CONFLICT|REJECT]
O4 OVERSIGHT   [PASS|HOLD|CONFLICT|REJECT]
CROSS EDGES    [PASS|HOLD|CONFLICT|REJECT]
-------------------------------------------
MULTI PASS     [PASS|HOLD|CONFLICT|REJECT]
```

A dramatic television cue may fire on `CONFLICT`, but the cue may not claim guilt, corruption, sexual misconduct, conspiracy, or criminality beyond what the evidence proves.

## Hand-Wave detector

The segment may identify a bounded rhetorical mismatch such as:

```text
QUESTION ABOUT X
→ RESPONSE ABOUT Y
→ X REMAINS UNANSWERED
= HOLD_NONRESPONSIVE_ANSWER
```

or:

```text
PUBLIC CHARACTERIZATION
!=
SOURCE-BOUND ORDER / TRANSCRIPT / RECORD
= CONFLICT_PUBLIC_ACCOUNT
```

`HAND_WAVE` is an explanatory label for a nonresponsive or scope-shifting answer only when the compared text supports it.

## FAFO television attachment

`FAFO = FOLLOW AUTHORITY, FOLLOW OUTCOMES`.

The final screen asks:

```text
WHAT DID THE INSTITUTION SAY?
WHAT DID ITS LAW ALLOW?
WHAT DID IT DO?
WHAT DID IT COST?
WHAT HAPPENED TO PEOPLE / SYSTEMS?
WHO REVIEWED IT?
WHAT WAS CORRECTED?
WHAT REMAINS OPEN?
```
