60 containers — not 60 FBI files

TESLA MUSEUM SOURCE

The Nikola Tesla Museum's official history states Tesla's legacy reached Rijeka in September 1951 in sixty packages, suitcases, metal trunks and barrels, aboard the ship Serbia. These were mixed physical containers, not sixty identical trunks.
