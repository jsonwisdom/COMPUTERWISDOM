## Public purpose

COMPUTERWISDOM exists to help Jason Wisdom and participating family members build replayable, evidence-bound civic and technical systems.

The repository preserves:

- family-originated builds and creative work;
- exact operator instructions and corrections;
- directories-first architecture;
- verifiable public-record research;
- auditable AI-agent actions;
- receipts showing what occurred, what did not occur, and what remains unresolved;
- privacy and consent boundaries for every person represented.

## Family-centered does not mean family-owned people

Family participation is part of the project's history and purpose. No family member is an asset, dependency, fictional character, authority token, or implied contributor.

A named person must have:

- a bounded relationship context;
- a consent status;
- an allowed-publication status;
- source-bound attribution;
- a correction or removal path.

Absence of consent means `HOLD`.

## Women in the build

Women must be represented as first-class participants, creators, builders, reviewers, subjects, or protected persons according to the evidence and their own boundaries.

The system must not:

- erase a woman's contribution;
- merge multiple women into a generic family role;
- invent consent or authority;
- publish private material because a repository is public;
- attribute Jason's statements to another person;
- speak or act on a woman's behalf without authorization.

## Public/private membrane

Public GitHub may contain:

- approved project manifests;
- public-source receipts;
- hashes and timestamps;
- code and tests;
- redacted instruction records;
- consented attribution;
- bounded civic analysis.

Public GitHub must not contain by default:

- raw private chats;
- children's or family members' private information;
- credentials or wallet secrets;
- medical, financial, or intimate records;
- unapproved statements attributed to another person;
- speculative allegations presented as fact.

## Backtracking duty

When prior AI labor disregarded or flattened an instruction, the repair record must identify:

1. Jason's exact ask and role at the original time;
2. the response actually produced;
3. the missing family, woman, directory, project, or constraint edge;
4. the compliance classification;
5. the proposed backfill;
6. Jason's approval;
7. the eventual artifact or commit.

Backfill repairs the present record. It never falsifies the past.
