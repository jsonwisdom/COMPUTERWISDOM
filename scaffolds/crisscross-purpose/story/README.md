## The story

Jason built across many chats, files, repositories, roles, places, and years.

Some pieces landed in the right directories. Some landed at the root. Some instructions carried forward. Others were flattened, separated, or forgotten.

CrissCrossPurposePatch does not pretend that history was clean.

It reaches across the builds and reconnects purpose:

```text
Safe Families
↔ Safe Neighborhoods
↔ Commonality
↔ Collaboration
↔ Publicity
```

The purpose is to help people find one another without taking ownership of one another.

It gives a doorway, not a border.

It gives choices, not assignments.

It gives credit, not authority.

It gives receipts when proof is required and leaves story as story.

## What this is

- a family-centered public story;
- a welcoming explanation of why the repositories connect;
- a shared purpose across COMPUTERWISDOM, AL, and JOY;
- an invitation to collaborate safely;
- a promise that people retain personal boundaries and escape routes.

## What this is not

- scientific evidence;
- a mathematical theorem;
- a political story presented as fact;
- proof that another person agrees;
- a claim over family members;
- legal, tax, residency, custody, or wallet proof;
- permission to expose private information.

## Commonality

People do not need identical beliefs, roles, families, neighborhoods, tools, or politics to share common ground.

Common ground may be:

- safety;
- dignity;
- useful work;
- clear boundaries;
- care for children and neighbors;
- honest correction;
- the freedom to participate or leave;
- public credit for chosen contributions;
- evidence when a factual claim affects others.

## Collaboration promise

A collaborator may:

- enter through one project without joining the whole system;
- contribute under a chosen name;
- limit the allowed use of a contribution;
- request correction;
- pause;
- fork approved work;
- export approved contributions;
- leave;
- choose another platform or community.

Collaboration never means automatic family membership, political agreement, public identity, or permanent access.

## Publicity promise

Publicity means making approved purpose and work visible.

It does not mean making people public.

Before publishing a person, family artifact, neighborhood detail, private conversation, image, voice, or identity link:

```text
ASK
→ PREVIEW
→ CONSENT
→ MINIMIZE
→ PUBLISH
→ READ BACK
→ KEEP CORRECTION AND EXIT OPEN
```

Missing consent means `HOLD`.
