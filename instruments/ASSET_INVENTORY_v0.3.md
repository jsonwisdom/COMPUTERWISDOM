# COMPUTERWISDOM Asset Inventory v0.3

Status: `DISCOVERY_INSTRUMENT`

v0.3 separates **artifact class** from **capability role**.

```text
ARTIFACT_CLASS = where/what the stored object is
CAPABILITY_ROLE = what the object does
```

A runnable validator may therefore be:

```text
artifact_class = executable
capability_role = instrument
```

This is not a multi-home conflict. Fixtures and proof outputs containing words such as `audit` or `verify` do not become instruments merely because of their names.

The instrument is read-only and heuristic. All classifications require review.

```text
MOVES_PERFORMED=false
AUTHORITY_CREATED=false
```
