Publication standard

Every published finding should provide a dated source, scope, acquisition method, actual receipt or nullable receipt field, human review status and correction channel. Classify PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN against a stated predicate; never use a score to imply authority.

Private and child-safety boundary: Do not submit child-abuse evidence, identifying details about minors, private health data or confidential case material to a public website or repository. Report urgent threats through appropriate authorities. Public research is not law enforcement.
