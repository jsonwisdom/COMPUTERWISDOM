## 1. Server Rules (Final)

**JAYWISDOM Rules**  
Directory First Building

1. **Directory First**  
   Everything has a place. Put it there. If it doesn’t have a home, make one before you keep going.

2. **Pre-Ready**  
   Ideas do not need to be finished. They need to be in the right directory so the next round can pick them up.

3. **Play First**  
   People come here to play, game, and create. Work and employment grow out of that. Never the reverse.

4. **Receipts Decide**  
   What you put in the directories is your record. Claims without placement do not count.

5. **Be Valuable to Yourself**  
   Leave with more structure, clearer work, and stronger capability than when you arrived.

6. **No Corporate Smell**  
   No empty motivation. No fake hustle. No performance. Keep it real and organized.

7. **Respect the Directory**  
   Do not dump. Place. The next person (or your future self) should be able to continue without cleaning up.
