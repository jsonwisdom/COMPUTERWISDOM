## Mechanics
Each round: fictional incident card; private uncertainty token; publicly recorded notify/inquire/silence actions; receipt challenge; independent check; scoring. Coordinator rotates A → B → C but obtains no special rights. Dimensions credibility C, transparency T, and timeliness R are bounded in [0,1], and weighted score is S=0.40*C+0.35*T+0.25*R. Escalation flag if fictional perceived hostility exceeds credibility and no notification occurs; in implementation a flag is **pending human review**, not autonomous operational escalation.

## Evidence & cryptographic constraints
PASS = checkable evidence accepted by independent verifier under the particular claim definition; FAIL = actual contradiction or defined protocol violation; UNKNOWN = missing, inaccessible, or unchecked proof. Missing evidence alone is UNKNOWN, not automatic proof of wrongdoing. SHA-256 authenticates byte equality to a trusted reference, **not provenance or real-world truth**. Require provenance, timestamps, challenge records, auditor identity, and human H2 review for evidence promotion.
