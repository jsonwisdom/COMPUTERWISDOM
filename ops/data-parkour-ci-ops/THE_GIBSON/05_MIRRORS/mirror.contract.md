# Gibson Mirror Contract V0.1

A mirror is a derived copy or representation of a source object.

## Rules
- source locator must remain recorded
- mirror creation must record transformation type
- mirror does not replace source
- mirror may be stale; replay decides MATCH|DELTA|HOLD
- cross-surface mirror does not merge authority
- public/restricted data membranes remain explicit

## OpenAI role
OpenAI-backed agents may summarize, classify, compare, propose routes, and generate candidate artifacts from declared inputs. Their output is a derived mirror unless separately verified against a source.
