
## Mission Card 001 — The Museum Sign

### Mission

A child sees a sign that says:

> Museum closes at 5:00 PM.

### 1 — What did I actually see?

> I saw a white sign with black letters on the front door.

Observation only. No meaning added.

### 2 — What am I guessing?

> I’m guessing the museum really closes at 5:00.

Inference, not yet a verified fact.

### 3 — Who said it?

> The sign was made by the museum staff.

This is a source claim, not a receipt.

### 4 — Where is the receipt?

> I don’t have one yet. I could check the website, ask the front desk, or call.

Receipts can be verification actions, not only physical objects.

### 5 — TRUE / FALSE / HOLD

`HOLD`

> I don’t know if the sign is current.

No accusation. No certainty. Pause at the unresolved edge.

### 6 — First Gap

> I don’t know when the sign was last updated.

That is the first unresolved gap.

## Parent-safe replay

Parent:

> You saw the sign. You made a guess. You noticed you don’t know when it was updated. That’s good thinking. Want to check together?

The parent models collaborative verification, not confrontation or authority.

## Takeaway

> I don’t have to believe everything I see. Checking is not rude — it’s how I learn.
