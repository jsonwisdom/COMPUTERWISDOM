## Episode replay cards

### 1. The Minnesota BOLO

A warning poster can look official and still be a joke.

Jason asks:

- What can I actually observe?
- What is rumor?
- What primary record would move the claim from `UNKNOWN` to `TRUE` or `FALSE`?

Gray Baby says: **official-looking is a visual property, not a provenance class.**

### 2. The Evidence Board

Red string, stamps, ranks, maps, and classified-looking labels are presentation.

Presentation is not proof.

Jason separates:

```text
OBSERVED_IMAGE_TEXT
from
VERIFIED_INSTITUTIONAL_FACT
```

### 3. The Wisdom Router

A diagram may describe an idea without proving the system is deployed, connected, authorized, or operational.

Jason asks for the implementation receipt:

`CODE → CONFIG → RUNTIME → OUTPUT → INDEPENDENT_REPLAY`

### 4. Ziggy in a Box

Packaging-like artwork does not establish that a product exists, is for sale, is safe, or has the listed features.

Jason asks for the real maker, manual, test, product record, or receipt.

`PACKAGE_ART ≠ PRODUCT_EXISTENCE`

### 5. The M on the Hill

A visual clue can start a local-history mission, but an image alone does not prove exact location, date, authorship, meaning, or institutional ownership.

Jason runs:

`OBSERVE → MEASURE → SOURCE → COMPARE → HOLD`

### 6. BoxD Sacred Cover

A seal can represent a rule without becoming a government seal, legal certification, or external authority.

“Truth • Constraint • Evidence • Decision” becomes a method only when another human can replay the receipt.

`SYMBOL ≠ AUTHORITY`

### 7. The Proof Forest

Schemas, checkmarks, PASS labels, and JSON-looking text are not executable proof by themselves.

Jason asks for:

- bytes;
- validator;
- inputs;
- outputs;
- version;
- independent replay.

`DIAGRAM_PASS ≠ RUNTIME_PASS`

### 8. “Trump Shock Gloves” poster

This image is treated as political satire and a claim-checking exercise. It cannot by itself establish a law, policy, procurement, deployment, budget, school rule, or enforcement authority.

Jason's move is smaller and stronger:

```text
NAME THE EXACT CLAIM
→ FIND THE ACTUAL STATUTE / POLICY / CONTRACT / TRAINING RULE
→ FIND THE AUTHORIZATION CHAIN
→ RECORD THE FIRST UNRESOLVED EDGE
→ HOLD
```

`SATIRE ≠ POLICY`

## Six-year-old replay loop

1. **SEE** — What can I actually observe?
2. **ASK** — What exactly are they claiming?
3. **CLASSIFY** — `FACT / STORY / SATIRE / SIMULATION / OPINION / UNKNOWN`.
4. **SOURCE** — Where is the primary record?
5. **GAP** — What is the first missing edge?
6. **TEST** — What evidence could resolve it?
7. **HOLD** — Do not invent the missing piece.
8. **RECEIPT** — Record what changed and why.
9. **SHARE** — Parent-safe first; provenance travels with the story.
