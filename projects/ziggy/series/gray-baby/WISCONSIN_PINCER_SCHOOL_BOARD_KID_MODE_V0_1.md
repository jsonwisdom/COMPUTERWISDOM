## Purpose

Use two independent evidence jaws to close inward on a school-board action without inventing the missing reason.

```text
LEFT JAW                         RIGHT JAW
PUBLIC DOCUMENT                  AUDIO / TRANSCRIPT
agenda / packet / website        meeting words / statements
        \                         /
         \                       /
          -> MOTION -> VOTE -> RESULT
                     |
                     v
             WHY / RATIONALE ?
                  = HOLD
```

The pincer is not an accusation tool. It is a fact-convergence tool.

## Core distinction

```text
WHAT HAPPENED != WHY IT HAPPENED
VOTE != MOTIVE
RECORDED_RATIONALE != PERSONAL_MOTIVE
ABSENCE_OF_REASON != PROOF_OF_BAD_REASON
```

A vote can establish an action. A transcript can establish words spoken. A public document can establish written proposals, recommendations, numbers, rules, or context.

None of those alone proves the private motive of a voter.

## Four evidence lanes

### 1. DOCUMENT LANE

Ask:

- What document is on the official website?
- What date/version is it?
- What exact proposal, amount, policy, contract, or recommendation does it contain?
- Is it an agenda item, packet, resolution, budget, contract, minutes, or another record?

State:

`DOCUMENT_FACT = SOURCE_BOUND | HOLD`

### 2. AUDIO / TRANSCRIPT LANE

Ask:

- Who spoke?
- What exact words are recoverable?
- Is the transcript human-confirmed against the audio?
- Did the speaker state a reason, condition, concern, recommendation, or objection?

Voice boundary:

`AUDIO -> TRANSCRIPTION -> DISPLAY -> HUMAN_CONFIRMATION -> INTENT`

State:

`SPOKEN_STATEMENT = OBSERVED | HOLD`

### 3. VOTE LANE

Ask:

- What motion was actually before the board?
- Who voted?
- When?
- What was the recorded result?
- Is the vote reproduced from direct minutes/action record, or only reported in an index/snippet/transcript?

State:

`MOTION = SOURCE_BOUND | HOLD`  
`VOTER = SOURCE_BOUND | HOLD`  
`VOTE_TIME = SOURCE_BOUND | HOLD`  
`RESULT = SOURCE_BOUND | HOLD`

### 4. WHY LANE

Separate two different questions:

**Recorded rationale:** What reason did the public record actually state?

**Personal motive:** What was inside a voter's mind?

```text
RECORDED_RATIONALE = SOURCE_BOUND only when stated in a replayable record
PERSONAL_MOTIVE = UNKNOWN_HOLD unless independently evidenced
```

A board member saying, "I support this because X," may source-bind a public rationale. It does not prove that X was the only private motive.

## Wisconsin Pincer replay

```text
DOCUMENT
  -> identify proposal / rule / number

AUDIO / TRANSCRIPT
  -> identify statements / reasons actually spoken

MINUTES / ACTION RECORD
  -> identify motion / vote / result

CRISSCROSS
  -> compare document, spoken record, and official action

FIRST GAP
  -> stop where the chain no longer has evidence
```

## Kid Mode questions

1. What does the paper actually say?
2. What did they actually say out loud?
3. What did they actually vote on?
4. Who voted, and when?
5. Did anyone actually say why?
6. Is that reason in the record, or are we guessing?
7. What is the first thing we still cannot prove?

## Fact-only boundary

```text
DOCUMENT_PRESENT != POLICY_EFFECT
AGENDA_ITEM != VOTE
VOTE != CERTIFICATION
VOTE != MOTIVE
TRANSCRIPT != PRIVATE_THOUGHT
OFFICIAL_LOOKING != SOURCE_VERIFIED
QUESTION != ACCUSATION
GAP != MISCONDUCT
DOUBT != DISPROOF
UNKNOWN_HOLD = VALID_RESULT
```

## Success condition

```text

SUCCESS != EXPLANATION_INVENTED
```

Kid Mode answer at the gap:

> "We know what the document says. We know what was said. We know who voted and when. We do not yet know why unless the record actually says why."
