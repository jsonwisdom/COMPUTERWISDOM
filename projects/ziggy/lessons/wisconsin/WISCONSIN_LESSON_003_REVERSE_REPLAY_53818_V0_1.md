## Reverse mission

Instead of starting with a claim and moving toward a receipt, Lesson 003 starts with the polished final artifact and walks backward until every dependency is visible.

```text
SHARED STORY / IMAGE
  <- PARENT-SAFE VIEW
  <- RECEIPT
  <- CLASSIFICATION
  <- GAP + ANOMALY LEDGER
  <- SOURCE / MEASUREMENT / OBSERVATION
  <- ORIGINAL QUESTION
  <- CHILD CURIOSITY
```

The reverse question is:

> What had to be true, sourced, measured, authorized, or explicitly labeled before this final thing could safely be shared?

## Wisdom reverse replay

```text
FINAL CLAIM
  -> WHAT EXACTLY IS BEING CLAIMED?
  -> WHO OR WHAT DOES IT REFER TO?
  -> IS IT FACT, STORY, SIMULATION, OPINION, OR UNKNOWN?
  -> WHAT SOURCE SUPPORTS IT?
  -> WHAT SOURCE IS MISSING?
  -> WHAT CHANGED FROM THE ORIGINAL?
  -> WHAT AUTHORITY IS IMPLIED?
  -> WAS THAT AUTHORITY ACTUALLY CREATED?
  -> CAN A PARENT / STUDENT / COMMUNITY MEMBER REPLAY IT?
```

- `CLAIM ≠ FACT`
- `DIGEST ≠ TRUTH`
- `ANCHOR ≠ AUTHORITY`

- `SCHOOL_REFERENCE ≠ SCHOOL_ENDORSEMENT`
- `FACEBOOK_POST ≠ PRIMARY_SOURCE`
- `UNKNOWN_HOLD = SUCCESSFUL_GAP_DETECTION`

## Delta10 applied to 53818

For this lesson, `DELTA10_53818` is a **local educational simulation label** for ten reverse-replay checks. It is not a representation of an official U.S. Space Force Delta 10 program.

1. **Claim** — write the exact claim.
2. **Object** — identify the person, place, number, image, policy, or event.
3. **Lane** — FACT / STORY / SIMULATION / OPINION / UNKNOWN.
4. **Source** — preserve the best available source.
5. **Time** — bind the claim to a date or period.
6. **Place** — bind geography without implying jurisdiction.
7. **Authority** — identify who could actually authorize the claimed action.
8. **Gap** — record the first unresolved dependency.
9. **Anomaly** — record conflicts, surprising differences, or impossible combinations.
10. **Replay** — give another human enough information to reproduce the reasoning.

`DELTA10_53818 = TEN_CHECK_REPLAY`, not military command authority.
